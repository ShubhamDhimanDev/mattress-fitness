# Risks and external dependencies

Created 2026-10-11; statuses refreshed 2026-10-15 from the build rounds up to 99fb950, the security reviews of 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15, DECISIONS (to DEC-049) and BLOCKERS (to B-21). Likelihood (L) and impact (I): H, M, L. Review at every phase gate. Each risk has an owner who acts on it; closed risks stay in the table with the closing note. **Everything built so far was exercised on mock.shop, fixtures, fakes or the local Extend mock; nothing has met a development store or Extend's demo host (B-13), so "mitigated in code" below never means "verified on Shopify".**
