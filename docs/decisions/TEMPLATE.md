# ADR-NNNN: Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided or must approve
- **Requirements:** REQ-/NFR- IDs this affects
- **Related:** questions, risks, other ADRs

## Context

The forces at play: the requirement, the constraint, the verified facts (cite [TECH_FEASIBILITY](../research/TECH_FEASIBILITY.md) IDs) and the unknowns. Separate fact from assumption.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two paragraphs. Imperative voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, new obligations, and what must be revisited if an assumption fails.

## Alternatives considered

Each option and why it was not chosen.

## Verification

How we will know the decision works (a test, a measurement, a review) and when it will be reviewed.

> Write a new ADR when a decision changes a system boundary, data flow, integration contract, hosting choice, dependency or framework version, or a security control. Do not edit an accepted ADR to change its meaning: supersede it. Add a one-line entry to [../../progress/DECISIONS.md](../../progress/DECISIONS.md).
