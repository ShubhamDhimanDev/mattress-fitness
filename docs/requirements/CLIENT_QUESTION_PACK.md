## Part 3. Things your team will do in Shopify, or approve

1. **Gift offers.** For each gift, create one automatic discount in Shopify that makes the gift item free, and **title it exactly as we agree** (our system matches on the title). Gifts must be their own product options that you do not also sell normally. Until the discount exists and we have tested it on your store, gifts stay switched off and no gift is ever shown as free.
2. **Bundle offers.** Same idea: one automatic discount per bundle, titled to match. Our pages show a saving only when Shopify itself reports it, so a wrongly titled discount means no saving is shown.
3. **Tag offer-only products.** Protection plans, the haul-away service, gift items and protectors are not meant to be found in search or bought alone. Tag them **offer-only** in Shopify so they stay out of search results and the sitemap. (Our checks already stop them being bought alone.)
4. **Keep the delivery ZIP list in two places.** The list is stored once for our site and once in Shopify's local delivery settings. They must be identical; we give you a check that compares them.
5. **Privacy policy.** Your policy should say that the site stores the ZIP code a shopper enters for delivery in a cookie on their device, and that order details are shared with Extend when a shopper buys protection. We do not write legal text; please have your legal adviser review it.
