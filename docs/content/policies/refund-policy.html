Returns & refunds

  • Approved refunds are typically received within 3 weeks. Refunds for purchases made by cash or check will be issued in the form of a check. Refunds for credit or debit card transactions will be credited back to the original card used for the purchase.

Order changes & cancellations

Before shipping:

  • Orders must be paid in full before processing and scheduling for delivery or pick-up.
  • Orders (including direct ship, custom, and special orders) may be canceled within 24 hours.

After shipping:

  • Cancellations are subject to approval by Customer Care and may include a restocking fee of 20% plus any delivery charges incurred.

Clearance & final sale items

  • All clearance and promotional items are final sale.
  • No returns or exchanges unless the item damage is noted at delivery.
