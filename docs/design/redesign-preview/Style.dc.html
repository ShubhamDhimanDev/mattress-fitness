Design direction

Bold, warm, easy to trust.

Heavy rounded headlines, one hero highlight, big friendly cards, red only for the brand and the main action. Inspired by the reference site's structure and feel, with our own colours, type and words.

Shape

Depth

Flat border for cards, soft lift for the finder and cart summary, deep shadow only for drawers and dialogs.

Space and layout

8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64. Page width 1400 px with a 40 px gutter (16 px on phones). Sections breathe 88 px on desktop, 44 px on phones.

Icons

22 px, 2 px rounded stroke, drawn in the project (no icon library). Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.

Motion

150 to 300 ms ease-out. Drawer slides in, progress bar fills, cards lift 2 px on hover. Everything stops under reduced motion. Nothing moves on its own, no auto-playing carousels.
